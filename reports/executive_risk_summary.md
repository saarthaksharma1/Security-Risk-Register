
# Executive Security Risk Summary

**Assessment Date:** November 1, 2026  
**Assessment Type:** Simulated GRC Control Assessment  
**Classification:** Fictional Portfolio Exercise

## 1. Executive Overview

A simulated security control assessment identified nine findings across identity and access management, vulnerability management, audit logging, change management, business continuity, and vendor security.

The assessment evaluated control weaknesses using a 5x5 likelihood-and-impact scoring methodology and documented remediation ownership, target dates, and evidence requirements.

The most significant outstanding risks involve vulnerability remediation, terminated-user access, privileged access approvals, and backup restoration testing.

## 5. Remediation Validation Highlight: F-001

Finding F-001 identified two terminated users with active application access.

In the simulated remediation exercise:

- Both identified accounts were disabled.
- A post-remediation access export showed no remaining exceptions among the two terminated users.
- One weekly HR-to-access reconciliation was documented.
- A remediation action log and control validation report were prepared.

**Current Status:** In Progress.

**Closure Decision:** Not recommended for closure at this time.

Additional weekly reconciliation evidence, reviewer approvals, and population coverage verification remain outstanding.

The residual risk remains High until the control's effectiveness is sufficiently demonstrated and reassessed.

## 6. Management Recommendations

1. Prioritize remediation of overdue High-risk findings.
2. Strengthen vulnerability management SLA tracking and escalation.
3. Formalize privileged account approval and periodic review requirements.
4. Verify that HR-to-access reconciliation operates consistently over time.
5. Retain backup restoration test results and recovery validation evidence.
6. Require documented evidence and validation before closing findings.

## 7. Conclusion

The simulated assessment demonstrates how control testing, risk scoring, remediation tracking, and evidence validation support risk-based security management.

While several existing controls reduce exposure, material risks remain unresolved.

Management should prioritize overdue findings and require documented validation before accepting remediation as complete.

## 8. Assessment Limitations

All findings, employee records, remediation activities, and business systems in this project are fictional. The assessment is a portfolio demonstration, not an audit of a real organization.

Results are based on a limited synthetic dataset and are not representative of actual enterprise security performance.
